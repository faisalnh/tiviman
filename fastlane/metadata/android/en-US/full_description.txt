Tiviman plays your own live-TV playlist on Android TV. It opens on a programme guide, and every screen works with the D-pad remote alone.

Tiviman ships no channels, subscriptions, or provider accounts. Add a playlist you already have the right to use.

<b>Sources</b>
<ul>
<li>M3U playlists by URL, Xtream Codes accounts, and Stalker portals (live TV only)</li>
<li>Several saved playlists, with rename, reorder, and a combined search</li>
<li>Playlists and credentials stored encrypted on the device</li>
</ul>

<b>Guide</b>
<ul>
<li>XMLTV import from a URL, an Xtream account, or the guide declared in an M3U file</li>
<li>24 hours of schedule to browse and search, with manual channel mapping</li>
<li>Optional refresh every six hours</li>
</ul>

<b>Player</b>
<ul>
<li>HLS, DASH, and progressive streams</li>
<li>Video, audio, and caption tracks, display modes, and a sleep timer</li>
<li>History, Favorites, channel search, and picture-in-picture</li>
<li>Experimental short recordings, two-stream multiview, and audio delay</li>
</ul>

No analytics, ads, or accounts. The app connects only to the addresses you enter. HTTP sources are allowed because most providers only serve HTTP.

Tiviman is in early development.
