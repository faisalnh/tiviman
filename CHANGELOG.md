## [0.1.0-beta.1] - 2026-11-02

First public build, published as a pre-release while testing on real TVs continues.

### Added

- Programme guide home screen with D-pad navigation, groups, History, Favorites, and channel search.
- Live-TV sources: M3U URL, Xtream Codes, and Stalker Portal, with several saved playlists and a combined browser.
- XMLTV guide import from a URL, a saved Xtream account, or an M3U-declared guide, with manual channel mapping and optional six-hour refresh.
- Player tracks, display modes, sleep timer, and picture-in-picture.
- Experimental foreground recording, two-stream multiview, and audio delay, within the limits in `docs/feature-batch.md`.
- Encrypted on-device storage for playlists and provider credentials.
